Services

You don't need to know exactly what you need. If something in your business feels harder than it should, start with the audit and we'll figure out the rest together.

Experience Audit

We walk through your business the way your customers and your team do, find the friction points, and hand you a clear plan with the simplest fix that will work.

Good if: things feel messy but you can't pinpoint why.
  • Walkthrough of how work and customers move through your business
  • A prioritized list of what's slowing you down
  • A recommended minimum viable solution you can act on right away

Build the Fix

Once we know what's broken, we build it. That could be a booking or intake flow, a website, a client tracker, automations, or a simple setup that ties your tools together.

Good if: you know the problem and want it handled.
  • Websites, forms, and intake or booking flows
  • Client, staff, and scheduling systems (Airtable, Notion, Google Workspace)
  • Automations that cut out repetitive follow-up

Ongoing Support

A system only helps if it keeps working. We stay on to make updates, train your team, and adjust things as your business grows.

Good if: you want a go-to person behind the scenes.
  • Monthly updates and fixes
  • Team walkthroughs and simple how-to guides
  • Check-ins to spot new friction before it piles up
